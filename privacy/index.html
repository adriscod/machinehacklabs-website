Privacy

Short skeleton for this draft site. Not a full legal policy.

Who we are

Machine Hack Labs is a CNC milling shop. Contact: quotes@machinehacklabs.com.

What the quote form collects

The quote form collects name, email, optional phone, job fields (material, quantity, due date, setups, tolerance class, notes), and the CAD file you attach. An estimate range is shown on the quote page and sent with the RFQ. That estimate is not a final bid and is not shown again on the thanks page.

Where it goes

On the live site the form posts to FormSubmit, which emails quotes@machinehacklabs.com. Local test submits stay in a shop inbox on the machine and are not emailed.

What this site does not collect

No cards, no payment details, no Chase account data. Payment happens on a payment link that arrives in the shop’s bid email, not on machinehacklabs.com.

Questions

Privacy questions: write quotes@machinehacklabs.com.
